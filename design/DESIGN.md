## Brand & Style

This design system defines the visual language for high-end luxury Indian weddings, combining bespoke entertainment curation with timeless editorial grace. Departing entirely from dark nightclub tropes, it embodies luminous royal warmth, refined modern celebration, and editorial distinction akin to high-fashion bridal anthologies.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
- **Movement:** Luxury Editorial Minimalist with Classical South Asian heritage nuances.
- **Atmosphere:** Sunlit, luminous, warm ivory courtyards, soft marigold-dappled shade, tactile paper textures, and deliberate editorial serenity.
- **Key Characteristics:** 
  - Expansive breathing space letting bespoke musical curations and architectural photography stand center-stage.
  - Delicate hairline rules (0.5px to 1px) reminiscent of custom gold foil letterpress.
  - High-contrast classical serif headings juxtaposed with architectural, functional sans-serif body typography.
  - Tactile warmth through layered shades of ivory, unbleached linen, soft sage, and muted champagne gold.

## Colors

The palette radiates gentle daylight and antique opulence, entirely free of synthetic club neons or stark blacks.

### Roles & Applications
- **Canvas & Surface:**
  - `bg-canvas` (`#FFFDF8`): Supreme background canvas evoking handmade cotton rag paper.
  - `bg-surface` (`#F8F4E8`): Warm ivory foundation for elevated cards, modal sheets, and soft container boundaries.
  - `bg-muted` (`#E9E0CF`): Subtle beige used for recessed panels, quotation blocks, and audio player track beds.
- **Accents:**
  - `primary` / `accent-sage` (`#AAB58C`): Muted pistachio and wild sage, used for interactive states, badges, and natural floral accents.
  - `secondary` / `border-gold` (`#D8C49A`): Sunlit champagne gold for bespoke badges, monogram dividers, active audio scrubbers, and luxury tags.
  - `tertiary` / `accent-sage-subtle` (`#DDE5C8`): Ethereal mint-sage wash for selected states, pill chips, and hovering feedback.
- **Typography & Structure:**
  - `text-primary` (`#1F1F1A`): Deep warm rich charcoal for high-contrast headlines and critical typography.
  - `text-secondary` (`#38382F`): Grounded earthy charcoal for standard body and narrative blocks.
  - `text-muted` (`#7A786B`): Olive-tinted mid-tone gray for metadata, timestamps, and captioning.
  - `border-delicate` (`#E4DAC8`): Whisper-thin border color providing structure without visual interruption.

## Typography

The typography couples the romantic, literary presence of `Playfair Display` with the balanced, geometric discipline of `Manrope`.

### Guidelines
- **Editorial Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Titles mimic high-art monographs and luxury invitations. Maintain loose line spacing to accommodate sweeping ascenders and descenders. Hero titles are set with a touch of negative tracking for a confident, bespoke feel.
- **Curated UI & Body (`Manrope`):** Clean and legible against organic backdrops. Extended line heights (1.6x - 1.7x) guarantee effortless readability for long-form wedding timelines, repertoire listings, and artist biographies.
- **Vogue Monograms & Meta Labels:** All categorical signposts, date trackers, and metadata leverage `label-caps` in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.14em`) to mirror gold-foil letterpress stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model favors architectural poise and asymmetrical balance over dense, utilitarian grids.

### Rhythm & Alignment
- **Grid Structure:** 12-column responsive fluid grid with wide gutters (`2rem` desktop) to enforce visual clarity between artist profiles, event narratives, and audio suites.
- **Macro Margins:** Outsized canvas padding (`4rem` desktop) creates a bespoke book-spread feel, isolating content frames within expansive ivory breathing room.
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - *Desktop (>1024px):* 12-column layout; hero sections employ offset column spans (e.g., 5-column editorial text beside a 7-column portrait frame).
  - *Tablet (768px - 1023px):* 8-column grid; margins contract to `2.5rem`, preserving negative space around media previews.
  - *Mobile (<767px):* 4-column layout; gutter softens to `1rem` and canvas margins to `1.25rem`. Complex compositions reflow strictly vertically with generous vertical stacking gaps (`space-lg`).

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain light, organic warmth, depth is established using delicate surface tonal shifts and warm, translucent shadows rather than harsh physical elevation.

### Depth Paradigms
- **Tonal Stratification:** Visual height is conveyed by moving from canvas (`#FFFDF8`) to elevated surface (`#F8F4E8`) to recessed inset (`#E9E0CF`).
- **Hairline Framing:** Borders replace heavy shadows. Containers, media cards, and separators feature 1px solid hairline frames colored with `#E4DAC8` or `#D8C49A`.
- **Sunlit Ambient Diffusion:** When overlays, dropdowns, or sticky music players demand detachment from the page plane:
  - Shadow configuration: `0 14px 34px -6px rgba(56, 56, 47, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(216, 196, 154, 0.12)`.
  - Shadows are tinted warm umber and muted gold rather than neutral cold gray.
- **Glassmorphic Champagne Layering:** Fixed navigation and bottom media controls use `rgba(255, 253, 248, 0.88)` with a `16px` backdrop-blur and a subtle `0.5px` bottom border of `#D8C49A` (30% opacity).

## Shapes

The design system embraces an architectural, tailored softness (Level `1` Soft). 

### Geometry Standards
- **Buttons, Form Inputs, and Tiles:** `0.25rem` (`4px`) corner radius. This evokes hand-trimmed archival stationery and deckled-edge wedding invitations.
- **Cards & Featured Folios:** `0.5rem` (`8px`) maximum corner radius to ensure media retains framing precision without harshness.
- **Pill Accents:** Genre badges, stage status pills, and interactive audio controls utilize fully rounded capsule profiles (`9999px`) to create an organic balance against geometric card boxes.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary CTA:** Deep charcoal background (`#1F1F1A`), crisp ivory typography (`#FFFDF8`), `label-md` font setting, letter-spacing `0.06em`, uppercase. Hover initiates a warm transition to `#38382F` with a soft champagne gold ring (`1px solid #D8C49A`).
- **Secondary CTA:** Warm ivory surface (`#F8F4E8`), hairline gold border (`1px solid #D8C49A`), text colored `#1F1F1A`. Hover transitions surface to muted pistachio tint (`#DDE5C8`).
- **Editorial Text Link:** Inline serif or tracked sans-serif text underlined by a `1px` gold rule with `4px` padding offset, animating width on hover.

### Tags & Editorial Chips
- **Acoustic / Genre Chips:** Compact pill shape, soft sage background (`#DDE5C8`), forest sage text (`#4B5438`), `label-sm` sizing, zero border.
- **Luxury Tier Badges:** Pill shape with `#FFFDF8` background, 1px border of `#D8C49A`, text colored `#7A663E` with `label-caps` typography.

### Cards & Media Portfolios
- **Performance Showcase Card:** Framed with `1px solid #E4DAC8`, background `#F8F4E8`, `0.25rem` radius. Media maintains a 4:5 editorial portrait aspect ratio with internal padding (`space-md`). Includes subtle gold headline, curated instrumentation tags, and an embedded quiet play button.
- **Testimonial Block:** Inset background (`#E9E0CF`), flanked by an italicized `Playfair Display` quote with a minimalist gold vertical accent rule (`2px solid #D8C49A`).

### Form Elements & Inquiries
- **Input Fields & Date Selectors:** Flat ivory background (`#FFFDF8`), bottom border only or fine hairline outline (`1px solid #E4DAC8`). Focus state illuminates a refined champagne border (`#D8C49A`) with no default browser glow. Placeholder text in `#7A786B`.
- **Selection Controls (Radios & Checkboxes):** Custom unadorned square or circular markers with `1.5px` border of `#AAB58C`. Checked state fills with `#1F1F1A` harboring a champagne-gold check or center point.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Bespoke Audio Experience Bar:** Floating docked bottom player in frosted ivory glass (`rgba(255, 253, 248, 0.92)`). Track progress represented by a hairline line in champagne gold (`#D8C49A`). Audio controls presented in deep charcoal (`#1F1F1A`) paired with live ceremony chapter markers (e.g., "Baraat Entry", "Sangeet Finale").
- **Ceremony Curation Timeline:** Vertical hairline path (`1px solid #D8C49A`) with alternating editorial milestones, stamped with sage metadata badges and serif chapter titles.
